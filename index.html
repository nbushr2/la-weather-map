Louisiana Live Weather

Click the map or a city marker to load nearest station observations + hourly forecast.
Turn layers on/off (Radar, Alerts, Boundaries) from the layer control.
Sources: NWS API (observations/forecast/alerts) + NWS MRMS radar WMS. Boundaries from your GeoJSON.
How “Current Conditions” work Observations come from the nearest reporting station (often an airport/ASOS). Forecast is gridded for the clicked point. Some rural areas may be far from stations.
